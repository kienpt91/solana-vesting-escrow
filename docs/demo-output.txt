   0           0     0.00%                   0
   3           0     0.00%                   0
   6           0     0.00%                   0
   7      233333    23.33%              233333
   8      266666    26.67%               33333
  10      333333    33.33%              100000
  14      466666    46.67%              233333
  15      500000    50.00%              266667
  20      666666    66.67%              166666
  29      966666    96.67%              466666
  30     1000000   100.00%              500000
  31     1000000   100.00%              500000

a revocation at day 20 after claims at days 7 and 15:
  vested 666666  already claimed 500000  to the beneficiary 166666  back to the authority 333334
  check: 500000 + 166666 + 333334 = 1000000

rounding: 10 tokens over 3 seconds vest as [3, 6, 10] (floor, never more than the exact amount)
